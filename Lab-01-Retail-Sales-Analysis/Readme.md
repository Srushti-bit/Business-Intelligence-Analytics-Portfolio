# Retail Sales Performance Analysis Using Excel

Problem Statement

Retail Sales Performance Dashboard for Executive Decision Making

A multinational retail company operates across several countries and sells
products through different customer segments. The management currently
receives raw sales data, making it difficult to monitor performance and
identify profitable regions, products, and customer groups.

The Business Intelligence team has been asked to design an interactive Excel
Dashboard that transforms the transaction data into meaningful business
insights. The dashboard should enable executives to monitor organizational
performance, identify opportunities for growth, and support strategic
decision-making through KPIs, slicers, charts, and dynamic visualizations.
The objective is to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments and business KPIs to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Analysis
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Dataset Information
- 4,115 order records
- 19 columns in the order dataset
- Customer, location, segment and order information

## Key Analysis
- Total Sales Analysis
- Total Cost and Profit Analysis
- Profit Margin Analysis
- Sales Performance by Region
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Executive KPI Benchmarking

## Business Value
This analysis helps management understand business performance, identify profitable products and customer segments, and monitor important performance indicators.
